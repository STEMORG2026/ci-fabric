# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be professional, be precise, and assume the other person is acting in good
faith. ci-fabric is a developer tool built for local, private use; the bar
for its community is the same as the bar for its gates: no silent failures,
no hidden costs, honest limits.

**Expected behavior**

- Keep technical disagreements technical; argue from evidence (logs, exit
  codes, diffs), not seniority or volume.
- Give and accept critique in the direction of the work, never the person.
- Respect the documented boundaries of cooperating projects: read, report,
  never modify another project's repository.

**Unacceptable behavior**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing other people's private information without consent.
- Deliberately misleading reports — fabricated results, overstated claims,
  or tests engineered to look green. In a CI tool, a false pass is not a
  style problem; it is the failure mode this entire project exists to
  prevent, and it is treated accordingly.

## Scope

Applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and reviews, and any
venue where the project is represented.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner (Sajan Gurung) at
**sajan.plg@gmail.com** with `[ci-fabric conduct]` in the subject. Reports are
reviewed confidentially; enforcement decisions are documented privately and
may include a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from project spaces.
Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself unacceptable.
